Risk Review Assistant
Client-side Risk Tool

Survey to Review
Comments, in Minutes

Upload a property risk survey PDF, extract and rate the parameters with Gemini, review the findings, attach supporting photos, then export a formatted Word review comments document. Use the chat bubble in the corner to dig into specific figures or scrutinize a parameter further — that conversation stays in the app and never enters the exported document. Your API key is never stored and must be re-entered each session, but your survey data, review edits, and photos are auto-saved in this browser and survive a page refresh — until you export or start a new review.
